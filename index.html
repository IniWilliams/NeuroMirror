Camera mode: move your index finger to aim and pinch thumb + index finger to collect a target. Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD to move, Space to collect. Mouse/touch: move and click or tap targets. You have 45 seconds.

Why does this matter?

Most interfaces rely on keyboards, mice or touchscreens. Gesture recognition is one alternative worth exploring, though it may not work well for everyone. Try both modes. Which is easier, more tiring, or more accessible for you?

Privacy: This demo does not upload or save camera frames, names, or scores. It loads the hand-tracking library and model from external servers. Camera access is optional and can be switched off by closing the page. It requires a secure HTTPS page or localhost.
